# Homepage wireframe visual capture list

October 1, 2026. The local homepage now follows the hospital-to-home Care proposal. Four generated concept photographs span the hospital, an unwell baby visit, a cheerful toddler checkup, and a young-child follow-up. Expressions vary across reassuring, cheerful, concerned, and attentive conversations. Each includes a screen-off iPhone. The scenes vary male and female doctors and include fathers, mothers, and both-parent visits. Care recaps, source answers, and caregiver records are labeled sample concepts. The two everyday tracking videos remain labeled placeholders.

Capture released product screens with fictional sample baby data. Make important text readable at phone width. Keep caregiver access distinct from the recording parent, and retain the actual source recording behind every finished demonstration timestamp.

| Slot | Asset required | Evidence and composition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hero slideshow | Four matching licensed or commissioned care conversations, with mobile compositions | Newborn hospital conversation with a phone on the mother's lap, followed by baby, toddler, and young-child visits with a phone near the exam bed. Quiet space for copy. The [current image brief](care-hero-image.md) documents the concepts, assets, and prompts. |
| First conversation | Hospital recap with questions and next steps | No tracking history required. Use a scripted conversation; clearly label sample data. |
| Care loop | Capture, review, shared next steps, next-visit brief | Follow one fictional family from hospital to home and the next checkup. Show the next visit receiving selected observations, rather than automatic medical interpretation. |
| Original words | Question answer and supporting local audio passage | Real source timestamp from the demo recording. Parent review visible. No fabricated reference on a manual note. |
| Caregiver follow through | Same summary, answer, and reviewed task on another phone | Caregiver receives derived records, without audio or enabled replay controls. |
| 01 Quick Log | Diaper, bottle, and sleep timer capture | Actual buttons, confirmation, and resulting timeline entries. A sleep timer is not a completed sleep interval. |
| 02 Voice catch-up | Spoken feed, result at original event time, then next event | Real interaction speed and review steps. Keep each result visible before the next description. |
| Optional device | Existing product photo plus a future button-to-entry capture | Use the actual Nubo device. Preserve accurate Bluetooth sync and Plus access descriptions. |

Forecast and insight demonstrations no longer occupy flagship homepage sections. Their broader product explanation belongs on the app page. Keep the former captures available for supporting product content.

The 14-day Plus preview starts when parents confirm that the child is here. Download and profile creation do not start it. Care remains upcoming and should not be represented as available at an imminent hospital stay.

Use responsive WebP assets and explicit dimensions. Important UI wording remains HTML. Finished demo videos should include captions, descriptive transcripts, pause and replay controls, and a reduced-motion fallback. No production video or interactive audio playback has been added to this wireframe.

The local preview has not been deployed.
