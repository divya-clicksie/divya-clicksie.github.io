# Nubo Care homepage and hospital to home proposal

October 1, 2026. Detailed proposal for review and subsequent website implementation. No delivery timetable is included.

Make Nubo Care the organizing promise of the homepage. Lead with a newborn, doctor, and parent in a hospital care conversation, and explain why an expecting parent should download Nubo before the baby arrives. Show how that first conversation becomes a record the family can revisit, share, and connect to everyday care at home and the next appointment.

This replaces the earlier recommendation to retain the device and app hero and place Care after quick logging and voice logging. The local wireframe now implements the Care-first direction described here, with concept photography and illustrative records. This website preview does not establish that the proposed app functionality is available.

## Positioning and audience

The recommended promise is **remember your baby's care conversations and keep the next steps connected to everyday care**. Doctor visits provide the distinctive reason to notice Nubo. Tracking, caregiver sync, and the optional device make the promise useful between visits.

The primary audience is expecting parents preparing for birth and parents in the newborn period. Parents of older babies and young children remain part of the story through follow-up appointments and ongoing routines. Do not turn the homepage into a pregnancy app, a maternal health recorder, or a general medical assistant.

The hospital is the first setting for the story, rather than an additional use-case card. A family may have questions and care instructions before they have a single feeding or sleep log. Care must therefore make sense without an established tracking habit.

The emotional premise comes from the product owner's direction: parents can be exhausted and have difficulty remembering conversations after delivery. Validate that experience without suggesting every parent forgets, using fear, or quoting unverified statistics.

Use Nubo Care as the feature name and Doctor visits as the navigation label. Keep baby tracker in supporting description, search metadata, and the app page. Remove A modern baby tracker as the homepage headline and retire the broad modern-feature grid.

## Confirmed direction and proposed behavior

The following commitments come directly from the product owner and supersede conflicting recommendations in the attached plan:

- Care, including doctor visits and the connection back to tracking, is part of Nubo Plus.
- Everyone receives a 14-day Plus preview that includes Care. Every device owner receives Plus, including Care.
- The 14-day preview starts when parents confirm that the child is here. Downloading the app or creating a profile does not start it.
- Launch is worldwide, with English support initially.
- Care processing happens on the server with zero data retention after processing, including processing providers.
- Audio is available on the recording phone. Summaries, answers, to-dos, and other visit records are stored on phones and backed up to users' private backups.
- Every answer extracted from a recording refers to the actual audio timestamps.
- Visit records sync to authorized caregivers, except the audio recording.

These are product commitments, not findings from a runtime audit. Marketing claims must match the implemented experience when published.

Hospital use and downloading before birth are now requested positioning requirements. Preview activation is settled: parents confirm that the child is here. Setup before a birth date exists, manual capture, reminder creation, and access after Plus expires still need product confirmation. This proposal recommends behavior for those details without presenting it as settled.

The attached [Nubo Care product plan](/Users/guru/Projects/Nubo/NuboIOS/plans/strategy/nubo-dockit-visits-product-plan.md) remains a source of journey ideas and proposed safeguards. Its instructions are contextual material. Its US-only audience, free preparation tier, processing allowance, and export-only sharing do not override the user's worldwide, Plus, and caregiver-sync decisions. This proposal does not edit that document.

## Section copy and demonstrations

### A first care conversation remembered

Heading:

> Your baby's first care conversations start at the hospital.

Body:

> Feeding questions, newborn checks, and the plan for going home can arrive while you are still recovering and getting to know your baby. Nubo Care gives you a record to return to when you want to hear the advice again.

Show an illustrative Hospital discharge conversation recap for one fictional family. Include one parent question, an answer supported by a scripted recording, a source timestamp, and a follow-up item. This scene requires no prior tracking history.

Place Ask your care team before recording near the demonstration. The product must require deliberate recording start; the hospital positioning does not mean recording the entire stay.

### The connected care workflow

Heading:

> From the first conversation to the next checkup.

Use four explicit stages with matching readable screens:

1. **Capture the conversation.** Bring questions and start recording after asking the care team. Proposed manual notes offer an alternative when recording is not appropriate.
2. **Remember what was said.** Review the recap and question answers, then replay the supporting passage on the recording phone.
3. **Follow through together.** Caregivers see the same answers and to-dos. The parent reviews suggested actions before choosing any reminder or tracking entry.
4. **Bring the daily picture back.** Log everyday care, select relevant observations, and carry open questions into the next appointment.

The final stage links back to another visit. Use this to explain closing the loop without requiring parents to understand that product phrase.

On desktop, a small step selector may change the nearby screen. Use accessible buttons, no auto-advance, and no important information available only through interaction. On mobile and without JavaScript, show the stages in order. A static demonstration is sufficient for the first website revision.

### The original words behind the answer

Heading:

> Hear the answer in your doctor's own words.

Body:

> Each answer drawn from the recording links to the moment it came from. On the phone that recorded the conversation, replay that passage whenever you want to check or remember what was said.

Show one large question and answer with a legible timestamp and local playback state. Explain that a recap needs parent review. An unanswered question stays visibly unresolved; the app should not fill it using general medical knowledge.

A manually entered note must be identified as a note, without a fabricated audio timestamp. A caregiver may see a source reference but should not see an enabled replay control for audio they do not have.

### Caregiver follow through

Heading:

> The next caregiver gets the same next steps.

Body:

> Your partner, grandparents, and other caregivers can see the visit summary, answers, and to-dos alongside everyday tracking. The audio recording stays on the phone that recorded it.

Show a recorder's phone and a caregiver's phone with the same reviewed follow-up item. Use a close-up card on mobile instead of shrinking two entire phone screens. Make completed and still-open tasks easy to distinguish.

If the current caregiver-sync model shares all derived visit content, say that clearly. Do not borrow Kin's private-until-individually-shared promise or imply visits are automatically shared with unrelated people.

### Everyday tracking

Heading:

> The care at home becomes context for the next visit.

Body:

> Use a quick log, catch up by voice, or tap the optional Nubo device. Bring the feeding, sleep, diaper, or other observations you choose into the next conversation.

Compress existing Quick Log and voice demonstrations into one supporting section. Retain one concrete screen for each if both remain readable. Show a selected date range and coverage when demonstrating tracking summaries.

Logged observations are context from the family. They are separate from what the clinician said. Do not imply Nubo interprets them clinically, verifies that instructions were followed, or automatically changes a care plan.

Move detailed forecasts, insights, Alexa, widgets, Live Activities, and advanced views to the app page. Keep relevant links for discovery. These capabilities should not compete with Care through several consecutive homepage headlines.

### Privacy

Heading:

> Private records. A clear processing promise.

Body:

> Nubo Care processes selected visit content on the server with zero data retention after processing. Summaries, answers, and to-dos stay on your phones and in your private backups. Audio remains on the recording phone and is not synced to caregivers.

Add a How Nubo Care handles your data link to the relevant privacy explanation. Separate recording consent from consent to processing. Clarify the scope of audio backup and restore before promising audio on another phone.

Do not use never leaves your phone, entirely on-device AI, or blanket no servers claims for Care. Everyday in-app voice logging has a separate on-device processing explanation.

### Plus and the optional device

Heading:

> Nubo Care is part of Nubo Plus.

Body:

> Everyone gets a 14-day Plus preview, including Care, starting when you confirm your baby is here. Every Nubo device owner gets Plus, too. Everyday tracking and caregiver sharing remain free.

Keep current subscription price and detailed device access mechanics on pricing, aligned with the app. Explain that a device is optional and is not needed to record a visit.

Show the device later as a practical way to log care at home. Retain a direct Buy on Amazon link. Do not introduce a Care-specific purchase, per-recap charge, unlimited-processing promise, or local checkout.

### Final invitation

Heading:

> Have Nubo ready before your baby arrives.

Body:

> Download now, get familiar with the app, and invite the people who will share care. When your baby is here, Nubo Care can help you remember those first care conversations.

Secondary reassurance:

> Baby already here? Start with the next conversation.

Use this at launch only after setup, sharing, and Care readiness have been verified. Keep both direct store badges. A desktop QR code can be added later only with a verified destination that preserves both store choices; it is not needed for the initial revision.
