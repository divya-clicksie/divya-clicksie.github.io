# Nubo Care helps families feel prepared for doctor visits and care at home

Practical support for remembering the conversation and knowing what comes next

Nubo introduces Nubo Care, helping parents and caregivers feel more prepared and confident caring for their child's health and development, with less to remember and piece together. The experience connects everyday observations with appointment preparation, a reviewable record of the doctor's guidance, and follow-through at home.

When a baby is awake through the night, a parent may have plenty to ask the pediatrician and little energy to reconstruct the week. During the appointment, listening, comforting the baby, and remembering instructions compete for attention. At home, another question can surface: what exactly did the doctor say to do next? Nubo Care gives families practical help through those moments.

Families can save a question when it occurs to them, record routines, or leave a voice observation. Nubo Care brings relevant history and potential questions into a visit brief that caregivers can review and add to. At the appointment, recording with permission gives them a conversation to return to afterward. The recap connects their questions with the doctor's answers and organizes the stated next steps. Answers extracted from a recording link to the relevant audio on the recording phone, so families can hear the original words again.

That support continues with the people caring for the child at home. A grandparent's observation can inform a parent's appointment. The reviewed answers and next steps can then help the grandparent care for the child the following day. Invited caregivers share child-care information, while raw visit audio stays on the recording phone and personal records such as private pumping remain private.

Nubo Care brings everyday tracking, useful views of recorded patterns, care information, visit preparation, and follow-up into one experience. The optional Nubo button device makes common routine events easier to capture. Families can begin with one question; they do not need a complete tracking history or a device to receive appointment support.

Privacy is built into how this help is delivered. Care records live on caregivers' phones, with backups in their own cloud accounts. Sharing is end-to-end encrypted. Most processing happens on phones; content that needs cloud processing is deleted afterward, with zero retention by Nubo and its processing providers. The family's saved record remains available to its caregivers.

The journey begins with hospital days and the first year, when routines and questions change quickly, and extends into the growing years. Nubo Care helps families organize observations and professional guidance. Its AI does not diagnose or answer medical questions; those conversations remain with the child's clinicians.

Nubo Care's launch home is [nubocare.app](https://nubocare.app/). The existing nubotracker.com website will redirect there, bringing the familiar tracking foundation into the broader care experience.
