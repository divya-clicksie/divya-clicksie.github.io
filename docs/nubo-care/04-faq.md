# Nubo Care questions for parents and caregivers

Preparing for appointments and following through at home

## Understanding Nubo Care

### What does Nubo Care help with

Nubo Care helps you feel prepared for your child's care, with less to remember and piece together. Save what you notice, bring useful questions to the doctor, return to what was discussed, and keep the next steps close at hand. The goal is practical help and more confidence through everyday care and appointments.

### Why would I use it if I already track feeds and sleep

A timeline helps you remember what happened. Nubo Care connects that everyday context with the questions you want to ask and the guidance you bring home. For example, selected feeding logs and a voice observation can help you explain a change at a visit. The reviewed answer can then help you remember what to do afterward.

### Who is it for

Parents and the people they invite to help care for their child, including grandparents and nannies. Someone caring alone can use the same preparation and remembering support. The child is the center of the record, and caregivers can contribute from the parts of the day they experience.

### Is the first year an age limit

No. The first year is the main marketing focus, including hospital days, coming home, and changing routines. The wider product direction extends through the growing years, when observations, development questions, documents, and appointments may become more useful than infant routine tracking.

### Do I have to log every day or invite someone

No. Begin with what matters to you: one question, a brief observation, or an upcoming visit. Tracking history can add context, but appointment support does not require a complete log. Inviting another caregiver extends the help when sharing is useful to your family.

### Does it replace advice from our doctor

No. Nubo organizes information around your child's professional care. Its AI does not diagnose or supply medical answers to your questions. It helps you bring those questions to the clinician and remember the answers they gave. Medical decisions stay with families and their healthcare professionals.

## Before during and after a visit

### How do I save something I want to bring up

Use the app to save a question, record an observation, or log a routine in the form that fits the moment. Everyday in-app voice logging runs on your phone. The expanded Tracker direction includes unstructured observations with extracted structure and tags that remain connected to the original note for review.

### How will appointment preparation use those records

The expanded Prep experience is intended to assemble a doctor summary and potential questions automatically from relevant recorded information. You can review what it includes, add context, and bring your own questions. It should help you explain what you noticed without requiring you to reconstruct the whole history yourself.

### What happens during the appointment

Current visit support lets you save questions beforehand and record the discussion with your care team's permission. Dedicated visit screens keep the conversation and resulting record together. You can also enter notes manually. A recording gives you source audio to return to; manual notes do not have audio references.

### How do I remember the answers afterward

Return to the visit recap, answers to your questions, and next steps. Each answer extracted from a recording links to its original audio timestamps. On the recording phone, you can replay the relevant passage to hear what was said. Review the organized record before acting on it.

### What if a question was not answered or the recap needs correcting

Keep an unanswered question for follow-up with the clinician. AI should not fill the gap with a medical answer. Check generated content against the available source and correct anything that does not reflect the conversation. The aim is a useful record of the guidance you received.

### What help is planned after the visit

Coordinator is intended to organize reviewed next steps and follow-up reminders, including medication reminders based on confirmed care instructions. It does not choose treatment or generate a dosing schedule. Later observations can become context for the next visit, so preparation begins with what happened since the last conversation.

## Help at home and as your child grows

### Do I need the Nubo device

No. The app supports everyday tracking and visit support on its own. The optional button device makes common routine events convenient to record when your hands are full. Remote is the working capability name for that device; its place within the umbrella does not make hardware a requirement.

### How do Insights and Forecasts help

Insights helps you see patterns in recorded information. Forecasts is the working capability for sleep and feeding estimates that help with everyday planning. A recorded pattern and an estimate serve different purposes. Neither should be presented as a diagnosis or a guarantee of what your child will do.

### What is Care Vault for

Care Vault keeps standing care details accessible, such as pediatrician information and important handoff context. The expanded scope includes insurance, emergency contacts, and care documents. Scanning discharge documents into reviewable information is a possible extension, with original documents retained alongside extracted content.

### What is Allergen Safestart

It is the working name for a proposed guided allergen-introduction journey. It should help families organize the experience and relevant observations, with pediatric review of the guidance. The final name and scope remain open. Individual medical questions belong with your child's clinician; the name must not imply a guaranteed safe outcome.

### Can I use Nubo during hospital days

Current visit support can begin with questions and a conversation recorded with your care team's permission, without a tracking history. The broader direction includes carrying hospital information into care at home. Detailed discharge and hospital-to-home workflows remain to be defined.

### Will the app still emphasize baby routines when my child is older

The proposed experience adapts to use. Infant routine controls can recede with your permission when they are no longer useful, while observations, care information, and appointments become more prominent. Earlier records remain available. This is a product direction, rather than a claim that every age-adaptive control is available today.

### Are Tracker Prep and Scribe separate apps

They describe capabilities within one Nubo Care experience. Families should encounter familiar actions, such as saving a question or reviewing a visit. The final public tool names remain undecided; you should not need to manage separate agents to move through your child's care.

## Caregivers privacy and access

### How does sharing make the support more useful

A grandparent can save an observation, a parent can bring it to the appointment, and another caregiver can use the reviewed guidance afterward. Shared child-care information syncs across invited caregivers on iPhone and Android. This carries preparation and remembering support to people who were not in the room.

### Does every caregiver receive the recording

No. Visit summaries, answers, next steps, and other shared visit records sync to invited caregivers. Raw visit audio stays on the recording phone. Personal boundaries also remain: private pumping records are separate from the shared child-care record.

### Where does the care record live

On caregivers' phones, with backups in their own cloud accounts. Nubo does not hold the persistent family care record. Caregiver sharing is end-to-end encrypted. The shared record remains useful to the people who hold it without becoming a central archive held by Nubo.

### Does any content leave the phone for processing

Yes. Most processing happens on phones, including everyday in-app voice logging. Current visit support sends selected content for server processing with zero retention afterward. The expanded architecture uses on-device models where practical and custom cloud models for tasks needing more capacity. Every new path must meet the same privacy requirement.

### What does zero retention mean

Content sent for cloud processing is deleted after processing by Nubo and its processing providers. The requirement includes temporary content copies, processing logs, and model systems handling that content. It does not delete the visit record saved on your phone or in your own private backup.

### Is the entire expanded experience available now

Nubo currently provides tracking, caregiver sync, visit questions, permission-based recording, recaps, audio-linked answers, and synced next steps. The wider product direction includes automatic preparation, broader documents, guided journeys, and age adaptation. The complete delivery scope of those expanded capabilities is still being finalized.

### Where will Nubo Care launch

The launch home is [nubocare.app](https://nubocare.app/). The existing nubotracker.com website will redirect there. Nubo Care is the working umbrella brand for tracking, appointment support, and follow-through. The domain transition does not by itself establish a release date or the availability of every expanded capability.

### How is current visit support accessed

Current Nubo Care visit support is included in Nubo Plus, the 14-day Plus preview, and device-owner Plus access. It is available worldwide with recording and recaps in English. The expanded umbrella's final packaging remains open; the rebrand alone does not establish new prices or entitlements.
