# Nubo Care overview

Practical support throughout child care

## The family benefit

**Nubo Care helps parents and caregivers feel prepared for their child's care, from everyday observations to doctor visits and what comes next.** It takes work out of gathering context, remembering conversations, and organizing the instructions families bring home. The intended emotional benefit is confidence and relief: less to hold in your head while caring for your child.

The child is the center of the experience. Parents, grandparents, nannies, and other invited caregivers are its users. A person caring alone can receive the same help without inviting anyone. The journey begins with hospital days, emphasizes the first year in marketing, and remains useful through the growing years.

## A familiar moment

A parent has noticed a change in feeding but cannot remember when it started. Their partner handled the night, and a grandparent noticed something during daytime care. The parent wants to explain the situation clearly at the pediatrician's appointment, listen to the answer, and remember the next steps afterward.

Nubo Care is designed to carry useful context through that sequence. Everyday observations become material for preparation. The doctor's answers become a record the family can review. Reviewed instructions remain available at home, including to the caregiver who was absent. Later observations help the family prepare for the next conversation.

## How the support works

**Capture what matters.** Use structured logs or a voice note for a routine, observation, development question, or something new your child did. Tap the optional device for common routine events when it suits the moment. A useful record can begin with a single question.

**Arrive prepared.** The expanded experience calls for an automatically assembled doctor summary and potential questions, using relevant recorded information. Caregivers review the brief, add context, and include their own questions. AI helps organize what to ask; the doctor supplies medical answers.

**Listen and remember.** Record with participants' permission, then return to a recap, answers, and next steps. Current visit support links extracted answers to the original audio timestamps on the recording phone. Review the record before acting and keep unanswered questions for follow-up.

**Continue at home.** Make reviewed guidance available to invited caregivers. Follow-up organization and reminders carry forward the clinician's instructions. New observations become context for another appointment.

## Capabilities within the same experience

The capability names describe the working product structure. Public names and the delivery status of expanded features remain to be finalized.

Tracker holds structured observations and voice notes. Remote is the working name for the optional button device. Insights helps families see patterns in recorded information; Forecasts offers sleep and feeding estimates for everyday planning. Estimates remain distinct from recorded events and professional guidance.

Care Vault keeps standing care details accessible, with broader document storage and extraction in the proposed scope. Allergen Safestart is a proposed guided introduction journey, subject to pediatric review and naming review. It must support questions for clinicians without implying a guaranteed safe outcome.

Prep organizes visit context and questions. Scribe organizes the conversation, with discharge-document scanning a possible extension. Coordinator organizes reviewed next steps and reminders, including medication reminders based on confirmed instructions. Families should encounter familiar actions such as saving a question or reviewing a visit, without having to manage separate agents.

## Coordination extends the help

Preparation is more useful when it includes what another caregiver noticed. Remembering is more useful when someone who missed the appointment can use the reviewed answers. Coordination belongs throughout the experience, well before a follow-up reminder is created.

Shared child-care information syncs across invited caregivers on iPhone and Android. Raw visit audio remains on the recording phone; private pumping records remain personal. These boundaries let useful care information travel without distributing every source or personal record.

## Privacy makes the experience trustworthy

Care records stay on caregivers' phones, with backups in their own cloud accounts. Nubo does not hold the persistent family care record. Sharing is end-to-end encrypted. Most processing happens on phones, including everyday in-app voice logging. Selected visit content uses server processing with zero retention afterward.

The expanded platform must apply the same zero-retention requirement to every cloud processing path, including temporary copies and processing providers. Deleting processing content does not delete the family's saved record. Privacy is a product requirement throughout the journey.

## The direction for the brand

Lead with practical support and relief. “Never forget what the doctor said” provides a concrete entry point into the wider promise of arriving prepared and knowing what comes next. Explain the care journey before the feature names, then show how coordination and privacy support it.

Nubo Care is the working umbrella brand, building on existing tracking and visit support. Its launch home is [nubocare.app](https://nubocare.app/), with nubotracker.com redirecting there. Broader hospital-to-home workflows, final tool names, and expanded delivery details remain open. Nubo supports care information and follow-through; medical decisions stay with families and their clinicians.
