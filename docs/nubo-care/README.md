## Shared direction

Lead with help for parents and caregivers: arrive prepared, understand and remember the conversation, and know what comes next at home. The emotional benefit is confidence and relief from having to remember and piece together scattered information.

Show how observations and questions connect to appointments, reviewed answers, follow-through, and the next conversation. Caregiver coordination extends this help across people. Uncompromising privacy governs how it is delivered. Neither sharing nor data ownership replaces the lead family benefit.

The child is the center, and parents and invited caregivers are the users. First-year marketing includes hospital days and coming home; the product direction extends into the growing years. Nubo Care is the working umbrella brand, with nubocare.app as its launch home and nubotracker.com redirecting there.

## Status and boundaries

The PR describes the intended launch experience in announcement language. It is a positioning exercise, not a statement that the expanded platform launched today. It has no invented launch date, customer quote, or endorsement.

The overview and FAQ distinguish existing foundations from expanded product direction. Current source code describes tracking, caregiver sync, visit questions, recording, recaps, audio-linked answers, and synced next steps. Later exploration answers specify automatic preparation, a unified app, custom processing models, and adaptation with user permission. These answers guide the direction without independently proving complete delivery.

Final public tool names, expanded packaging, and detailed hospital-to-home workflows remain open. AI organizes context and clinician guidance; it does not diagnose or answer medical questions. Shared child-care information syncs, raw visit audio stays on the recording phone, and private pumping remains personal.

The six-page narrative is outside this rewrite and has not been updated. It is not part of the current three-document pack.
