Coming home with a premature baby can mean learning a new routine while carrying a discharge plan, follow-up appointments, and very little sleep. The useful question is not how to record everything. It is what you and the people helping need to remember before the next feed, handoff, or appointment.

Nubo can hold everyday care records alongside your observations. Start with the details your baby's care team has asked you to track. Their instructions determine the care plan; the app helps you keep the record.

## Start with the discharge plan

Before building a logging routine, identify what the team wants recorded and who will review it. A feeding time, an amount offered, and an amount actually taken are different details. Ask which matters for your baby's plan, and preserve that distinction in your entries and notes.

Set up the child profile, check the birth and due dates, and invite the caregivers who will help at home. Give everyone the same small set of logging instructions. If a detail needs explanation, keep the original wording in an observation rather than squeezing it into an unrelated event.

Paper can remain the right place for a discharge checklist. The shared app is useful when the next caregiver needs to see what has happened since they last helped.

## Keep dates and growth measurements in context

A premature baby's calendar age and corrected age describe different things. The American Academy of Pediatrics explains how corrected age accounts for the weeks a baby was born early when considering development. Ask your team how to use it for your child. <a href="https://www.healthychildren.org/English/ages-stages/baby/preemie/Pages/Corrected-Age-For-Preemies.aspx" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Read the AAP explanation of corrected age</a>.

Nubo supports preemie/Fenton growth tracking. Record measurements from visits with their dates and units, then use the chart as a conversation aid. The AAP lists Fenton among its <a href="https://www.aap.org/en/patient-care/newborn-infant-and-early-childhood-nutrition/newborn-and-infant-nutrition-assessment-tools/preterm-infant-growth-tools/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">preterm growth assessment tools</a>. Your clinician should choose and interpret the appropriate reference for your baby. A percentile in an app does not establish whether growth is adequate.

## Make the next handoff specific

Rather than asking someone to read the whole timeline, review these together:

- The last recorded feed and any note about what was actually taken.
- Recent care events the team asked you to record.
- A question or observation that needs to reach the next caregiver.
- Any gaps where care happened but an entry is missing.

Nubo Intelligence's in-app voice logging can help you catch up on supported events. Select the correct profile, say what happened, then review the times and amounts before saving. Missing logs remain unknown; a total only describes the care that was recorded.

## Bring the questions as well as the numbers

Before a follow-up, choose the entries you want to discuss and write down the question behind them. For example: “Which feeding details would you like us to bring next time?” or “Which chart and age should we use when reviewing these measurements?”
