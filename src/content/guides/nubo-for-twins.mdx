You remember making two bottles. You are less sure which baby finished theirs, who woke first, and which change your partner handled. With twins or multiples, the hard part of tracking is often attaching the right detail to the right child while everything happens close together.

Nubo's free app supports multiple child profiles and unlimited caregivers. Each child gets their own care history. The routine below keeps those records useful when two people are helping at once.

## Give each child a separate profile

Create a profile for each baby, using names that every caregiver recognizes. Check the birth and due dates for each profile rather than assuming every detail should be copied from one sibling to the other.

Invite the people who need access to each child's timeline. An invitation to one child's record is not a substitute for setting up access to the other. Before the first shared shift, have each helper open both profiles and find the last feed in each.

## Check the profile before the event

Make the child's name the first thing you check when logging. Then record the event and confirm it appears in that child's history. The same check matters when entering a past event or reviewing an amount.

For a tandem feed, keep a separate entry for each baby. Sharing a feeding time does not mean both babies took the same amount or nursed for the same duration. If you only remember one amount, keep the other uncertain instead of copying it to make the timelines match.

This is also the rule for Nubo Intelligence's free in-app voice logging: select the intended child first, speak the supported events, and review before saving. Do not rely on saying both names to route one catch-up across two profiles.

## Hand off one child at a time

An effective handoff can be short. Work through the same questions for each baby:

1. What was the last recorded feed, and what detail does the next caregiver need?
2. Is a sleep or nursing timer still running?
3. What happened since the other caregiver last checked?
4. Is anything missing from the log, or waiting to be discussed?

If two adults share a task, agree who will log it. That small decision helps avoid duplicate entries. When someone catches up later, check the timeline first so the same event is not added twice.

Paper labels or a note beside the feeding supplies can still help everyone identify belongings. Nubo's role is to keep the recorded care for each child available to the people you invite.

## Keep appointments specific to each child

Prepare separate questions and observations for each baby, even if they share an appointment. Nubo Care, included in Nubo Plus, helps you return to recorded answers and shared next steps after a visit. Review the recap with each child's needs in mind rather than assuming advice for one applies to both.

If either baby was born early, use the [preemie guide](/guides/nubo-for-preemie-babies/) alongside the care team's instructions. As routines change, the [toddler guide](/guides/nubo-for-toddlers/) can help you reduce the amount you track.
