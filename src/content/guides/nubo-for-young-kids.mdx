At a checkup, someone asks how things have been going. You remember wanting to mention something after pickup last month, but the wording has gone. Caring for a young child still creates details worth keeping, even when bottles and diaper counts are no longer part of the day.

This guide focuses on the preschool years, roughly ages three to five. Nubo's role can shift toward observations, milestone records, vaccine history, and questions you want to bring to a visit. You can use those parts without maintaining a newborn logging routine.

## Capture one concrete example

An observation is easier to discuss later when it says what happened and when. “Told me the whole story of their day at pickup” preserves more than “language is improving.” A question about a recurring difficulty is more useful with a dated example than with a label you are not sure fits.

Use Nubo Observations to preserve a spoken or typed observation. Keep your child's words where they matter, and distinguish what you saw from what someone else reported. You do not need a daily journal. Save the details you expect to want again.

Nubo Intelligence helps organize what you save in Nubo Observations. Original observations stay available on supported app platforms; AI titles and tags require iOS 26 or later with Apple Intelligence available. Other devices keep the text with a simpler title and no AI tags.

## Use milestones to prepare a conversation

Nubo's milestone records include age groups through five years. They can help you remember examples of skills you have seen and questions you want to ask. An unchecked item is a reason to have a conversation, rather than a diagnosis from an app.

The CDC provides <a href="https://www.cdc.gov/act-early/milestones/5-years.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">milestone information for five-year-olds</a> and explains that its checklists do not replace standardized, validated developmental screening. Discuss concerns with your child's clinician. You do not need to wait for a complete app record before asking for help.

## Keep each child's history separate

If there is a younger sibling at home, use a separate profile for each child. The preschooler's observations and visit questions should not disappear among the baby's feeds. Check the child's name before adding an entry or sharing a detail with another caregiver.

Invited caregivers can use the same child's record across iPhone and Android. Choose who needs access to the shared history. Nubo's caregiver timeline is for people you invite, so a school or childcare provider does not receive it automatically. See [how caregiver sharing works](/caregivers/).

## Follow through after the appointment

Before a visit, select the examples you want to discuss and turn each into a question. Afterward, the useful record is often small: what was discussed, what you agreed to try, and what you need to ask next time.

Nubo Care, included in Nubo Plus, supports visit preparation, recording with permission, audio-linked answers, and shared next steps. Review the recap against the conversation before relying on it. Summaries, answers, and to-dos sync to invited caregivers; original audio stays on the recording phone. Selected visit content uses requested server processing with no retention afterward. Keep vaccine entries as a personal reference alongside the official records from your child's provider. Your app history can help you find a question for the next appointment without becoming the place you manage every part of school life.
