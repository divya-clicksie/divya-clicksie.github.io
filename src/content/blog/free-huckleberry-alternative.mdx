It is hard to compare baby apps when you are already tired of logging. If you are looking for a **free Huckleberry alternative**, start with this: Huckleberry and Nubo both let you track everyday care for free. The reason to try Nubo is the way your household logs together, especially when a shared account, an open phone, or a missed entry slows you down.

## The short answer: which app fits your day?

- **Choose Nubo Free** if several people log care and you want each invited caregiver on one shared timeline. Quick logs and offline, in-app voice catch-up are included, so you can tap now or say what happened later.
- **Choose Nubo Plus** if you also want automatic reminders, iPhone Live Activities, CSV exports, or private pumping records. It is an optional app upgrade at $39.99 per year.
- **Keep Huckleberry** if its sleep guidance is the reason you open the app. SweetSpot nap predictions and Insights are part of its paid Plus plan; Premium adds personalized sleep plans and Berry guidance.

Huckleberry's <a href="https://huckleberrycare.com/product/free" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">free plan</a> already includes feeds, diapers, sleep, pumping timers, reports, reminders, and caregiver sync. Its <a href="https://huckleberrycare.com/pricing" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">pricing page</a> currently advertises Plus at $5.74 per month billed yearly and Premium at $9.99 per month billed yearly, with the more advanced sleep tools in those paid plans.

## Why try Nubo when Huckleberry is also free?

Picture a handoff: one parent logs a bottle, a grandparent logs a wet diaper, and the next caregiver checks the same baby timeline. Nubo lets you invite caregivers without asking everyone to use one sign-in. Huckleberry also syncs between caregivers, but its <a href="https://huckleberrycare.com/product/free" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">sharing instructions</a> say to sign in to the same account on each phone. If you would rather invite people separately, that difference matters more than another feature on a list.

Now picture a feed you forgot to log while settling the baby. Nubo's free voice catch-up lets you say what happened after the fact, even when your phone is offline. Huckleberry offers text, voice, and photo AI logging with Plus or Premium. If all you want is a quick way to catch up without subscribing, Nubo gives you that path. See [how voice catch-up handles missed logs](/blog/tracking/catch-up-baby-logging-after-the-fact/).

There is also the phone itself. You can use Nubo as a free app with quick logs, then add the optional [one-tap Nubo device](/device/) if opening a screen at 3 a.m. becomes the problem. Device taps, app entries, and voice catch-up feed the same timeline. You do not need the device to use Nubo.

## Where Huckleberry may be the better fit

Huckleberry's paid sleep tools answer a different question: when might the next nap work, and what should you try if sleep is hard? SweetSpot predicts sleep timing for babies two months and older, and Premium adds tailored sleep plans. Nubo tracks sleep and can show patterns, but it does not offer a SweetSpot-style sleep prediction. If that guidance is helping your family, keep using it. Read [why Nubo does not predict your baby's sleep](/blog/product/why-nubo-does-not-predict-your-baby/) for more on that choice.

Huckleberry Free also includes a pumping timer. In Nubo, the baby's bottle of expressed milk goes in the free shared feed log; your own pumping sessions and milk output use the private tracker in Nubo Plus. That distinction matters if pumping is part of your daily record.
