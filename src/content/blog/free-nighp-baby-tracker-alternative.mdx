When the last feed feels like it happened yesterday and five minutes ago, a tracker should make the answer easy to find. If you are looking for a **Nighp Baby Tracker alternative**, Nubo is worth trying for its free app without ads, offline voice catch-up, and shared caregiver timeline. Baby Tracker by Nighp also keeps core tracking free, so the better choice depends on how your family logs.

## The short answer: which app fits your family?

- **Choose Nubo Free** if you want feeds, sleep, diapers, growth, medicine, and caregiver sharing without ads. Quick logs and on-device voice catch-up are included, even when your phone is offline.
- **Choose Nubo Plus** if you need private pumping records, automatic reminders, CSV exports, or iPhone Live Activities. It costs $39.99 per year as an optional app upgrade.
- **Keep Baby Tracker by Nighp** if its pumping logs, Apple Watch, Siri Shortcuts, or new What’s Next timing cues are part of your routine. Those features give you reasons to stay with the app you already know.

Nighp's <a href="https://apps.apple.com/us/app/baby-tracker-newborn-log/id779656557" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">US App Store listing</a> says core tracking stays free. It lists a $4.99 one-time ad-removal purchase and Baby Tracker Plus at $5.99 per month or $49.99 per year. Plus includes ad-free tracking and personalized timing cues after a three-day preview. Nubo's free app has no ads or trial deadline for core tracking; [Nubo Plus pricing](/pricing/) is separate from the free app.

## What feels different in everyday use?

Both apps cover the basics and sync records across devices. In Nubo, you can invite a partner, grandparent, or nanny into the same baby timeline. Someone logs a bottle, someone else logs a diaper, and the next person can see the day without a text recap. The choice is less about whether sharing exists and more about how you want to make entries.

For the times you remember an event late, Nubo's free in-app voice catch-up lets you say what happened in plain English. The processing runs on your phone and works offline. Quick logs are there when tapping is easier. If you want to log with your phone across the room, the optional [one-tap Nubo device](/device/) can add feeds, sleep, and diapers to that same timeline after it syncs. The app works on its own, with no device purchase required. See [how voice catch-up handles missed logs](/blog/tracking/catch-up-baby-logging-after-the-fact/).

Nighp has a different kind of convenience. Its current <a href="https://apps.apple.com/us/app/baby-tracker-newborn-log/id779656557" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">app listing</a> describes Apple Watch entries, Siri Shortcuts, Live Activities, pumping logs, PDF reports, and its What’s Next sleep, feed, and diaper timing cues. If you use a watch to log or depend on those timing cues, staying with Nighp may be the easier choice.

## Compare the paid extras only if you need them

Nubo Free can record the bottle of expressed milk your baby drinks on the shared timeline. Tracking your own pumping session and output is a private Nubo Plus feature. Nighp lists pumping logs in Baby Tracker's feature set, so a parent who pumps regularly should check that difference first.

Nighp's What’s Next cues are an optional paid feature. Nubo Plus adds tools such as reminders, exports, and private pumping, but it does not predict the next feed or nap. Read [why Nubo leaves sleep prediction out](/blog/product/why-nubo-does-not-predict-your-baby/) if that difference matters to you. If you mainly want an ad-free shared log with voice catch-up, start with Nubo Free.
